The calendar of your custody arrangement

Week on, week off, 2-2-5-5, 2-2-3, alternate weekends or your own pattern. Official school holidays for your zone (A, B or C), first half on even years, Mother's and Father's Day weekends, one-off swaps.

The bag that travels

The cuddly toy, the treatment, the sports kit: see what is where, and get a checklist before every handover.

Shared expenses

Who paid what, 50/50 or your own split, photo of the receipt, and a clear balance: who owes how much to whom.

Health and treatments

Allergies, doctor, treatments with times: each parent ticks the dose given, the other sees it instantly.

Blended families

As many homes as needed, and a schedule per child: two dads, two mums, everyone has their place.

Help

Getting started

Create your family, add your homes and children, choose the custody pattern and your school zone. Then invite the other parent from the More tab: they receive a link, open it on their iPhone, and everything is shared.

Questions

Write to us at moonbeamstudio.contact@gmail.com, we answer every message.

Privacy

Deux Toits stores your data on your device and in your own iCloud account. When you invite another parent, the family is shared between your iCloud accounts through Apple's CloudKit sharing. We, the publisher, have no access to it: there is no server of ours, no account, no advertising and no tracking. Reminders are local notifications created on your device. You can stop sharing or delete your data at any time from the app or from your iCloud settings.
