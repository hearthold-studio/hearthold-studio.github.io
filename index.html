What we believe

Built like good furniture

Solid, honest, pleasant to use for years. That's the bar for every game and every app in the house.

Craft first

Small teams, carefully finished products. We'd rather make fewer things, and make them well.

Honest by design

No dark patterns, no forced ads, no account when none is needed. You always know what you pay for.

Made to last

Our games and apps keep being maintained and improved long after launch.

For everyone

Designed in France, available in several languages, built to be accessible to all.
